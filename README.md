A project aiming to make initial size estimates for some of the bolides detected by the Geostationary Lightning Mapper (GLM) on the Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellites GOES_16, GOES_17, and GOES_19. The first
part of the project will involve assuming a density for the bolide and then using the 3D column of air it passed through coupled with the generalization that the bolide with catastrophically destruct when it encounters a 
column of air roughly equal to its own mass to estimate its radius. The second will involve using equations taken from a paper (Hueso et All 2018, Small impact on the giant planet Jupiter) to analyze the lightcurves of the 
bolides and extract an inital size estimate from them. This project involves a lot of biases (noted below) and is meant to be an initial estimate ONLY.
